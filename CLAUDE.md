# Claude Code Workspace Instructions

## Canonical Policy

Before reviewing, generating, or editing any file in this workspace, read and follow [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) in full. It is the single source of truth for resume content, candidate history, formatting, ATS requirements, metric calibration, file handling, and quality checks.

Do not copy or restate the complete contents of `AGENTS.md` in this file or another document. When instructions conflict, follow the user's current request first, then `AGENTS.md`, then this compatibility file.

## Candidate Integrity

- Preserve the role titles, employers, and dates recorded in `AGENTS.md` Section 9.
- Never invent production systems, tools, certifications, projects, responsibilities, or metrics.
- Treat unverified numbers and MLOps claims as validation items, not established facts.
- Keep claims realistic for approximately two years of experience and prioritize production infrastructure over modeling.

## Required Workflow

1. Inspect relevant workspace files before proposing changes.
2. Research current job descriptions and authoritative industry sources before making time-sensitive ATS or hiring-market claims.
3. Preserve useful content and provide a stronger replacement for anything removed.
4. Apply the five-stage engineering formula or Google XYZ structure to every resume bullet.
5. Verify the result against the quality checklist in `AGENTS.md` before reporting completion.
6. Update the edited document's `## Changelog` when its content materially changes.

## ECC and Superpowers

- Use relevant ECC skills for domain-specific research, writing, review, and verification when they are available in the active agent environment.
- Use relevant Superpowers process skills before implementation, including brainstorming for new designs, systematic debugging for defects, test-driven development for code changes, and verification before completion.
- Read each selected skill's `SKILL.md` completely before acting, announce its use, and follow its required approval or verification gates.
- Skills supplement the workspace policy; they do not override `AGENTS.md` or the user's instructions.

## Resume Delivery Gates

Before delivering a resume or resume-source change, confirm:

- The section order matches `AGENTS.md`.
- The final resume remains a single-column, one-page document.
- The PDF is text-selectable and ATS-readable.
- Every bullet has a quantified, factually verified impact and satisfies the required engineering formula.
- Forbidden verbs are absent.
- Dates, titles, links, skills, certifications, and metrics are consistent across source files and generated outputs.
- The output filename follows `FIRSTNAME_LASTNAME_MLOps_Resume.pdf`.
- No placeholder, unsupported claim, or copied `AGENTS.md` content remains.

## Safety and Scope

- Do not modify unrelated files.
- Do not delete strong content without an improved replacement and explicit justification.
- Do not publish, submit, upload, or send resume materials without direct user authorization.
- Report blockers and unverifiable claims clearly instead of guessing.
