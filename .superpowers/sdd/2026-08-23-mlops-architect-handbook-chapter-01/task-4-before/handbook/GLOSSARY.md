## Entry contract

Each controlled row records a term, concise definition, aliases, source key, verification state, and scope note. The source key must resolve to [the bibliography](sources/references.md). `Verified` is reserved for a definition whose cited source supports the exact wording; a handbook-specific definition is labeled `Handbook synthesis` and carries its assumptions in the notes.
