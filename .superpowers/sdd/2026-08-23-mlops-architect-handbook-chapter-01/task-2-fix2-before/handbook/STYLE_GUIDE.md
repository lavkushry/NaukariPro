# Handbook Style Guide

## Voice and audience

Write for Senior, Staff, and Principal MLOps / ML Platform Architects in a direct engineering voice. Lead with a system, artifact, incident, diagram, or decision; explain the reasoning after the concrete context.

## Structure

Use ATX headings and this hierarchy: one chapter `#` title, `##` major sections, `###` subsections, and `####` only for necessary detail. Expand acronyms at first use and keep each section focused on one purpose.

## Evidence and claims

Record claims in the claim ledger. Label architectural judgment as synthesis, distinguish scenarios from observed facts, version-pin volatile product behavior, and avoid unsupported superlatives.

## Diagrams and examples

Keep diagrams text-based and editable. Every diagram requires an identifier, caption, purpose, assumptions, provenance, text alternative, and a semantic review of directionality and responsibility boundaries.
